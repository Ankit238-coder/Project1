# Electric Vehicle Market Performance & Insights

## 1. Executive Summary
This project analyzes market dynamics across 20 global electric vehicle models spanning two-wheelers, sedans, SUVs, buses, and commercial haulage. The analysis delivers key findings into manufacturer sales volume, battery chemistry adoption, energy efficiency, and regional manufacturing hubs.

## 2. Key Findings & Strategic Insights
- **Market Leadership:** Tesla and BYD lead global EV volume, with SUVs accounting for over 45% of total adoption.
- **Battery Landscape:** Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) batteries dominate high-volume mass-market vehicles and public transit buses for thermal safety and low cost, whereas Nickel Manganese Cobalt (NMC) powers high-performance sedans and heavy commercial trucks.
- **Range & Efficiency:** Two-wheelers and compact sedans achieve maximum energy efficiency (~6.5 km/kWh), providing maximum driving range per battery footprint.
